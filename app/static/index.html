Zamp case study · Invoice processing

An invoice arrives. What happens to it?

A mid-size company gets hundreds of supplier invoices a month, all as PDFs. Someone opens each one, finds the matching purchase order, checks the numbers, and decides what to do. It's slow, and Friday afternoon mistakes are expensive.

This does that job end to end. It reads the PDF — including scans with no text in them — matches it to a purchase order, runs seventeen checks, and produces one of four decisions with its reasoning attached.

The interesting partMost costly mistakes aren't visible inside a single invoice. A duplicate looks fine alone. So does a third delivery that overruns its order.
Where the AI isThe model reads documents. It doesn't decide anything — every rule is ordinary code, so it can be tested and explained.
